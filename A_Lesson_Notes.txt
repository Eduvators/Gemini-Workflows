A Lesson Notes

Fictional workshop teaching notes, created for practice.
A claim is a statement someone asks us to accept. Evidence is information offered to support that claim. A polished website is not evidence that a claim is true. When evaluating an online claim, identify the author, inspect the evidence, consider the date and context, and compare with another reliable source. An advertisement may provide useful facts, but its purpose is to sell. A personal story may describe one experience without establishing what usually happens. If the evidence is missing, record what you still need to know rather than guessing.
