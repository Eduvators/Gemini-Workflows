C Practice Scenario

Fictional scenario for evaluation, not a real product or research finding.
A website selling a study app says: “Our app doubles learning speed.” Its evidence is a quote from one satisfied user. The page has no publication date, no research report and no description of how learning speed was measured. A learner should identify the seller as the source, recognize the testimonial as limited evidence, and ask for independent research and a clear measurement method before accepting the claim.
